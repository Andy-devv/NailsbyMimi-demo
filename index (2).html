Policy

Good to Know

Please read before your appointment so we can make the most of our time together.

🌸

New Clients

  • Please remove any existing nail products before your appointment for a fresh start.
  • A $20 deposit is required to secure your booking.
💅

Nail Health

  • Please do not book if you have an active nail infection, open wound, or condition affecting the nails or surrounding skin. Your appointment may be rescheduled if necessary.
  • Food is not allowed. Beverages are welcome.
  • Please do not bring additional guests unless they also have a scheduled appointment and have informed me in advance.
  • Please keep conversations at a comfortable volume out of consideration for others in the home.
🕐

Cancellations & Lateness

  • Please provide at least 24 hours notice for any cancellations or rescheduling.
  • Arrivals more than 15 minutes late may need to be rescheduled or may result in a shortened service.
💳

Payment

  • Payment is required upon completion of your service.
  • Accepted methods: Venmo, Zelle, or cash. Cash preferred.
